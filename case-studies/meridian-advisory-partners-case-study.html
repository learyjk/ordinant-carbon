Meridian Advisory Partners

Professional Services March 15, 2022

Inconsistent Regional Reporting

Meridian Advisory Partners struggled to get consistent reporting from its regional offices. Each office interpreted submission requirements differently, and corporate sustainability staff spent more time clarifying instructions than reviewing actual data.

What Meridian Needed

  • A single set of templates every office could follow
  • Clear deadlines with automated reminders for overdue submissions
  • A review workflow that preserved regional context while centralizing approval

“We were managing reporting through email and hope. That wasn’t sustainable.” — Harriet Colby

Meridian began evaluating platforms that could support multi-office professional services firms without forcing every region into an identical operating model.
