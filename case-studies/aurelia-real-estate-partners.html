property development draft 0000-00-00 00:00:00

Pilot Overview

Aurelia Real Estate Partners is piloting supplier evidence tracking across three active developments. The firm wanted to test whether a centralized platform could replace the mix of shared drives and email attachments its project teams had relied on for years.

Early Observations

Although the pilot is still underway, Aurelia has already seen meaningful improvements:

  • Project managers submit evidence through standardized checklists
  • Missing documentation is flagged automatically rather than discovered late
  • Corporate sustainability staff review all three sites from one dashboard
  1. Select three developments with differing supplier profiles
  2. Configure evidence categories aligned to Aurelia’s disclosure framework
  3. Evaluate completion rates and review time over two reporting cycles

“Even at pilot scale, the difference in visibility is noticeable.”

Aurelia plans to expand the rollout to its full development portfolio pending results from the current pilot phase.
