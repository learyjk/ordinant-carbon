Crestpoint Service Solutions

Multi-Location Services July 14, 2021

60+ locations standardized

Franchise Reporting Chaos

Crestpoint Service Solutions operates across more than 60 franchise locations, each previously reporting data differently. Some franchisees emailed spreadsheets; others uploaded files to a shared drive that no one monitored consistently.

Standardizing at Scale

Ordinant Carbon Solutions gave Crestpoint a franchise-ready reporting framework:

  • Franchisees submit evidence through standardized checklists
  • Regional franchise directors review before corporate consolidation
  • Corporate tracks completion rates across all 60+ locations in real time

“Sixty locations meant sixty versions of the truth. We needed one.” — Griffin Ashdown

Rollout Highlights

  1. Pilot with ten franchise locations to validate field workflows
  2. Train regional directors on review and approval routing
  3. Deploy firm-wide with automated reminders for overdue submissions

Crestpoint now maintains 60+ locations standardized under a single reporting process.
