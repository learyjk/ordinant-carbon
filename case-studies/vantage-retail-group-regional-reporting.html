Vantage Retail Group Gets Consistent Regional Reporting

Multi-location Services April 19, 2022

Consistent reporting across all locations

Dozens of Locations, Dozens of Processes

Vantage Retail Group needed consistent reporting from dozens of individually managed retail locations. Store managers treated sustainability reporting as a corporate afterthought, submitting data late and in formats that were difficult to consolidate.

A Tiered Review Model

Ordinant Carbon Solutions introduced a workflow that matched Vantage’s operating structure:

  • Store managers submit utility and waste data through mobile-friendly forms
  • Regional managers review submissions before they reach corporate
  • Corporate sustainability staff approve final figures from one dashboard

“Store managers needed something simple. Regional managers needed visibility. Corporate needed control.” — Solange Mbeki

Outcomes

  1. Every location now submits through the same system
  2. Regional review catches errors before corporate consolidation
  3. Reporting completion rates improved across all regions

Vantage now achieves consistent reporting across all locations, with a process that scales as the group opens new stores.
