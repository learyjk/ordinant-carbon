Bellwether Education Trust Case Study

Education September 8, 2022

A Growing Network

Bellwether Education Trust needed to bring reporting into line across a growing network of schools. As the trust acquired new institutions, each arrived with its own reporting habits, vendor relationships, and documentation standards.

Building a Consistent Foundation

Ordinant Carbon Solutions provided a scalable framework for the trust’s expanding portfolio:

  • New schools onboard to shared templates during acquisition integration
  • Trust administrators review all submissions from a central dashboard
  • Automated reminders keep schools on schedule ahead of disclosure

“Every new school used to mean a new reporting headache. Not anymore.”

Rollout Approach

  1. Standardize evidence requirements across existing schools
  2. Embed Ordinant onboarding into the new-school integration checklist
  3. Review consolidated trust-wide figures quarterly before board reporting

Bellwether is now positioned to scale its sustainability reporting as the network continues to grow.
